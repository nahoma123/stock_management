# Sales & Demos

Use this guide to qualify prospects, prepare demonstrations, and describe the product accurately without needing engineering documentation.

## Product position

The Stock Platform is a managed inventory and sales system for companies that want a dedicated Odoo environment without operating the infrastructure themselves. It is strongest for businesses that need product catalogs, stock visibility, sales workflows, reporting, spreadsheet onboarding, and room for controlled customization.

## Discovery questions

Ask the prospect:

- How many locations, warehouses, and users do you have?
- How do you identify products: machine code, model code, barcode, or another reference?
- Where is your current product and opening-stock data stored?
- Do you use ETB, USD, or both?
- Which sales and inventory reports are essential?
- What must differ from a standard installation?
- Who approves data imports and workflow changes?

## Demo preparation

Before a demo:

- Use a demonstration tenant, never a customer production tenant
- Load representative products and stock
- Confirm the tenant is active and monitoring is healthy
- Test the login and the exact workflow you plan to show
- Prepare one product import example and one reporting example

## Safe commitments

You can present separate tenant environments, managed provisioning, stock and sales workflows, spreadsheet product onboarding, central monitoring, and reviewed tenant customization as current capabilities.

Do not promise a delivery date for a customization, integration, billing feature, mobile feature, or infrastructure scale target until product and engineering have reviewed it.

## Sales handoff

Provide product and operations with the customer name, desired subdomain, number of users and locations, sample spreadsheet, required workflows, requested customizations, target date, and decision-maker. Clearly label statements as confirmed requirements, preferences, or open questions.
